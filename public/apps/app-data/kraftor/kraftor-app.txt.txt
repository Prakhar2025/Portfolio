Kraftor App — End-to-End Features List
The Kraftor App is a complete React Native (Expo) mobile application that syncs live with the main website's database. It offers role-based access for the general public, registered clients, employees, and administrators.
Below is the end-to-end breakdown of all features available in the app.
---
1. Public Features (Unauthenticated Users)
Anyone who downloads the app can access these features without logging in:
Home Dashboard: Live hero banners, featured offers pulled directly from the database, and quick navigation links.
Services Directory: Detailed pages for all 6 core services (Graphic Design, Web Development, App Development, Video Editing & VFX, Digital Ads, AI & ML Solutions) including pricing and package details.
Portfolio Showcase: A gallery of past projects and client work.
Packages & Pricing: Breakdown of standard and premium service packages.
Special Offers: A live feed of active discounts or promotions set by the admin.
About & Crew: Company background, vision, and team member profiles.
Contact CRM: A direct contact form that sends messages straight into the Admin CRM system.
Careers Portal: A job application form where candidates can apply for openings. Applications are routed directly to the Admin HR dashboard.
Authentication System: Secure Email/Password Login with a "Forgot Password" reset flow.
---
2. Client Portal (Authenticated Customers)
When a client logs in, they are automatically routed to a dedicated portal to manage their projects and billing:
Client Dashboard: A high-level overview of active projects, pending invoices, and recent files.
Project Tracking: Clients can view the status of their ongoing projects, including phase progress and completed tasks.
Project Chat: Real-time chat system for communicating directly with the Kraftor team regarding specific projects.
File Vault: A secure area where clients can view and download files, assets, and deliverables shared by the admin.
Billing System (Invoices & Quotations):
View detailed invoices and quotations.
Accept or decline pending quotations directly from the app.
Meeting Booking: Schedule virtual or physical meetings with the Kraftor team.
---
3. Employee Portal (Staff & Team Members)
Employees have their own restricted workspace to manage their daily work and HR needs:
Employee Dashboard: A quick overview of today's tasks, recent announcements, and attendance status.
Attendance System:
"Clock In" and "Clock Out" functionality.
Captures the work mode (Remote/Office) and location when clocking in.
Task & Project Management: Employees can see which projects they are assigned to and check off tasks as they complete them.
Project Chat: Collaborate with clients and admins on assigned projects.
HR Desk:
Submit leave requests (Sick, Casual, Paid).
Upload important documents (ID proofs, medical certificates) securely.
View documents shared directly by the HR Admin.
Salary Slips: View and download monthly payslips with a full breakdown of earnings, deductions, and bonuses.
---
4. Admin Panel (Full System Control)
Business owners and administrators have full control over the entire ecosystem:
Business Dashboard: Real-time metrics showing total revenue, outstanding payments, and pending approvals.
User Management:
View, edit, and manage all user accounts.
Create new Client or Employee accounts directly from the app (without being logged out of the admin session).
Project Management: Create new projects, assign them to employees and clients, and track overall progress.
Billing Engine (Invoices & Quotes):
Generate custom invoices and quotations with automated sequential numbering (e.g., `KFR2607IN001`).
Send invoices directly to clients.
HR & Payroll Management:
Review and approve/reject employee leave requests.
Monitor daily employee attendance logs.
Generate and distribute monthly salary slips.
Upload secure files to specific employee profiles.
CRM & Leads Management:
Read and reply to Contact Form submissions.
Review leads generated by the website's AI Chatbot.
Recruitment Desk: Review job applications submitted through the Careers portal.
Client Vault Management: Upload deliverables and files securely to a client's private vault.
Meeting Manager: Review, confirm, or reschedule meeting requests from clients.
Marketing: Publish and unpublish "Special Offers" that appear on the Public Home screen.
